What it does

You save your receipt numbers. The app looks up the case status from official sources and shows it to you clearly.

All your cases together

In your language

Case status arrives in official wording. We explain it in plain words, in four languages.

A notice when it changes

If your case moves, the app tells you. No need to check every day.

How it works

1

Enter your receipt number

It is the number printed on the notice that came to you in the mail.

2

We do the lookup

The request goes out from our server to the official sources, never from your phone.

3

You see the status

With the date, what it means, and what usually comes next.

What it does not do

It is not affiliated with the government. TramiTrack is a private, independent service. No United States government agency sponsors or endorses it.

It does not ask for more than it needs. You do not have to create an account with a name and password, and we do not ask for your Social Security number to work.
